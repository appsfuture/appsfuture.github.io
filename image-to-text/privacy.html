Privacy policy

Image to Text: Scan & Read (iPhone) · Last updated 7 October 2026

This policy explains what the Image to Text app ("the app"), made by NeeApps (Neelam Kumari), collects and how it is used. You don't need an account to use the app.

Photos, documents and text

  • Text is read from your photos and PDFs on your iPhone. Your photos and files are not uploaded to us.
  • When you translate text, or listen to it with the online voice, that text is sent to Google Cloud (Translation and Text-to-Speech) to get the result. It is not stored by us.
  • Your scans and translations are saved in History on your phone only. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • The app asks for camera, photo and microphone (speech-to-text) access only when you use those features.

Advertising

Free users see ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, rough location (from your IP address) and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads.

  • The app asks for your permission before tracking (Apple's App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form from Google lets you choose how your data is used for ads.
  • Pro users don't see ads.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand which features are used, and Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix crashes. These collect device and app information (such as device model, iOS version, app version and anonymous usage events), not your scanned text.

Purchases

Pro subscriptions and point packs are bought through Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether a purchase is active.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

  • Turn off tracking any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Remove camera, photo or microphone access in Settings → Image to Text.
  • Stop all data collection by deleting the app.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
