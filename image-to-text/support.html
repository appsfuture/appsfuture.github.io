Common questions

How do I scan text?
Tap Scan on the Home screen, then take a photo or pick an image or PDF. The text appears in a few seconds and you can copy, share, translate or listen to it.

Why is some text missing or wrong?
Use a clear, well-lit photo where the text is sharp and fills most of the picture.

What are points?
Free users spend points to scan. You can get more points in the app, or go Pro for unlimited scans.

How do I cancel Pro?
Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Image to Text and tap Cancel Subscription.

I bought Pro but it is not active.
Open the app, go to Go Pro and tap Restore while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Where are my scans saved?
In History, on your phone only. Deleting the app deletes them.
