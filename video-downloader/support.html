Video Downloader – Story Saver by NeeApps

How to download

  • Any link: copy the link of a public video or photo, open the app, tap Paste, then Download and pick a quality.
  • Facebook / Instagram: open the Browser tab, open the public video or reel, play it, then tap Download.
  • Status: watch the status in your chat app, then open the Status tab and tap the download button.

Where are my files?

In your gallery: Movies › Video Downloader (videos) and Pictures › Video Downloader (photos), and in the app's Downloads tab.
