Privacy policy

Video Downloader – Story Saver (Android, com.neeapps.videodownloader) · Last updated 25 November 2026

This policy explains what the Video Downloader – Story Saver app ("the app"), made by NeeApps (Neelam Kumari), collects and how it is used. You don't need an account to use the app.

Links you paste or share

  • When you paste or share a link, the app opens that public web page from your phone to find the video or photo files on it, and checks their size and format.
  • Links are not sent to us or stored on any server. Analytics only records the website name (for example "vimeo.com"), never the full link.
  • Downloaded files are saved on your phone only, in your gallery (Movies or Pictures › Video Downloader). The list in the Downloads tab is kept on your phone only.

In-app browser

  • The Browser tab works like a normal web browser. If you log in to a website there, you log in on that website's own page: the app never sees or stores your password.
  • Cookies and website data are stored on your phone only, like in any browser, so you stay logged in. They are not sent to us.
  • When you tap Download, the app reads the current page to find the video files on it. Nothing from the page is uploaded to us.

Status Saver

  • Status Saver only reads the folder you choose yourself with Android's folder picker (the statuses you already viewed in your chat app). The app does not ask for access to all your files.
  • Statuses are only copied to your gallery when you tap Save. Nothing is uploaded.

Analytics and crash reports

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes.

  • They collect device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, app version and crash logs) and anonymous usage events (such as which screens are opened, whether a download worked, and the website name).
  • They do not collect your files, the links you open, your browsing history or your passwords.
  • Firebase Remote Config is used to turn features on or off and to tell you when an app update is available.
  • This data is not used to identify you.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, rough location (from your IP address) and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form from Google lets you choose how your data is used for ads.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID any time in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Some ads are optional: you can choose to watch a short video ad to get extra downloads.

Purchases

The app offers an optional subscription (Video Downloader Pro) through Google Play. It renews automatically until you cancel, and can be cancelled any time in Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether a purchase is active.

Content and copyright

Only download content you own or have permission to use. The app does not support YouTube. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, X or any other website or app.

Google

Firebase and AdMob are provided by Google. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

  • Remove folder access any time: Settings → Apps → Video Downloader → Storage → Clear data, or choose a different folder in the app.
  • Delete downloaded files from the Downloads tab or your gallery.
  • Delete the app to remove all data saved by it on your phone (downloaded files in your gallery stay until you delete them).

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
