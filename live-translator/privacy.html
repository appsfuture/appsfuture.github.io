Privacy policy

Live Translator (Android & iPhone) · Last updated 8 November 2026

This policy explains what the Live Translator app ("the app"), made by NeeApps (Neelam Kumari), collects and how it is used. You don't need an account to use the app.

Microphone and speech

  • The app uses your microphone only while you tap the mic to speak. It does not listen in the background.
  • While you speak, the audio is sent to Google Cloud Speech-to-Text to turn it into text. The text is then sent to Google Cloud Translation, and the translation is sent to Google Cloud Text-to-Speech so it can be read aloud.
  • We do not store your audio, your words or your translations on any server. Google processes them only to return the result.
  • Your conversation is shown on your phone only and is cleared when you close it or tap "Clear conversation".

Settings

Your choices (languages, voice, speed and similar) are saved on your phone only.

Analytics and crash reports

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes.

  • They collect device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, app version and crash logs) and anonymous usage events (such as which screens are opened).
  • They do not collect the words you speak, your translations or your audio.
  • This data is not used to identify you.

Advertising

The free version of the app may show ads from Google AdMob in a future update. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, rough location (from your IP address) and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form from Google lets you choose how your data is used for ads.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID any time in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • On iPhone, the app will ask your permission before tracking, and you can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Purchases

If the app offers a subscription, it is bought through Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iPhone). We never see your payment details. The store tells the app only whether a purchase is active.

Google

Speech, translation and voice are provided by Google Cloud. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

  • Remove microphone access any time. On Android: Settings → Apps → Live Translator → Permissions. On iPhone: Settings → Live Translator → Microphone.
  • Delete the app to remove all data saved on your phone.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
