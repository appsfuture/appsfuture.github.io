Privacy policy

Live Translator (Android) · Last updated 8 November 2026

This policy explains what the Live Translator app ("the app"), made by NeeApps (Neelam Kumari), collects and how it is used. You don't need an account to use the app.

Microphone and speech

  • The app uses your microphone only while you tap the mic to speak. It does not listen in the background.
  • While you speak, the audio is sent to Google Cloud Speech-to-Text to turn it into text. The text is then sent to Google Cloud Translation, and the translation is sent to Google Cloud Text-to-Speech so it can be read aloud.
  • We do not store your audio, your words or your translations on any server. Google processes them only to return the result.
  • Your conversation is shown on your phone only and is cleared when you close it or tap "Clear conversation".

Settings

Your choices (languages, voice, speed and similar) are saved on your phone only.

Ads, analytics and purchases

The app has no ads, no analytics or tracking, and no in-app purchases.

Google

Speech, translation and voice are provided by Google Cloud. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

  • Remove microphone access any time in Settings → Apps → Live Translator → Permissions.
  • Delete the app to remove all data saved on your phone.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
