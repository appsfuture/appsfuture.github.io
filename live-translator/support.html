Live Translator (Android)

Live Translator lets two people talk in different languages. Tap the mic, speak, and hear the translation instantly.

Common questions

  • It stops before I finish speaking. Open Settings and increase "Pause before stopping".
  • No sound. Check your phone volume and that "Read translations aloud" is on in Settings.
  • It says no internet. The app needs an internet connection to translate.
